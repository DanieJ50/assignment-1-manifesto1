From Shy to Bold

  • Small
  • in New York noise,
  • I folded my voice
  • like a paper star.
  • Texas opened sunlit doors.
  • Now I design
  • with louder color,
  • moving memory into motion,
  • fear into navigation,
  • softness into signal.
  • Each click becomes proof:
  • the quiet girl
  • was never gone—
  • she was loading
  • into boldness.
